Narada

One explorer for your files, network, phones and drives — the same on Windows, macOS, Linux and Raspberry Pi.

Download · v0.1.2

Windows

Installer (.exe) .msi package

macOS Intel & Apple Silicon

Disk image (.dmg)

Linux x86-64

.AppImage .deb .rpm

Raspberry Pi 64-bit · ARM64

.AppImage .deb .rpm

macOS is signed & notarized by Apple — it just opens. Windows isn't signed yet, so SmartScreen may warn: More info → Run anyway. Linux/Pi: make the AppImage executable (chmod +x) or install the .deb/.rpm. All builds and checksums are on the releases page.

What it does

One record per system

Every device on your network and tailnet, found through every channel it offers.

Verified transfers

Copy preview, pause/resume, and a SHA-256 check on every file.

Phone drop

AirDrop-style sharing to any phone by scanning a QR code — no app to install.

Drives & dual boot

Another operating system's disks, read safely and read-only.

Play media

Video and music stream from anywhere, with tags and cover art.

Built-in terminal & probes

Your shell, plus ping, traceroute, DNS and more — no admin rights.
