ToggleMail

One email app for all your accounts. Free, no ads, open source — a family project by Toggle Minds.

See it

Try the demo in your browser Made-up mail, nothing to install.

What it does

  • PhoneMail (just your number and password), Gmail, Outlook, iCloud, Yahoo, Zoho, Proton, mailbox.org, Posteo and more — or any IMAP / POP3 / SMTP account
  • Accounts grouped by service, each with its own folders and unread counts
  • Inbox, Starred, Snoozed, Drafts, Scheduled, Sent, Archive, Spam, Trash and your own folders
  • Schedule an email for later; snooze one until you need it
  • Safe HTML reading: no scripts, pictures only from senders you allow
  • HTML writing with attachments, links and signatures
  • Reading pane on the right, at the bottom, or off; navigation as icons
  • Passwords kept in your computer's keychain, never on our servers

Flatpak

Any Linux with Flatpak (x86_64). Download the file, then:

flatpak remote-add --user --if-not-exists flathub https://dl.flathub.org/repo/flathub.flatpakrepo

flatpak install --user ToggleMail_0.1.2_x86_64.flatpak

.flatpak

Coming to Flathub soon: we will be submitting ToggleMail there shortly. Until then, install this file; to update, install the next version’s file the same way.
